# 🏙️ Municipal Complaint Management System

A software engineering project designed to provide a centralized digital platform for citizens to **register, track, and manage municipal complaints** and for municipal authorities to **monitor, assign, and resolve civic issues efficiently**.

> **Project Status:** 📋 SRS & Project Report Completed — Development Phase Pending

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Municipal authorities receive complaints related to common civic issues such as garbage collection, damaged roads, drainage problems, street lights, water supply, and other public services.

Traditional complaint-handling processes can make it difficult for citizens to track their complaints and for authorities to efficiently manage and prioritize them.

The **Municipal Complaint Management System** aims to solve this problem by providing a centralized platform where citizens can submit complaints and monitor their progress, while municipal officials can manage complaints through an organized system.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

* Provide citizens with an easy way to register municipal complaints.
* Allow citizens to track the status of their complaints.
* Maintain a centralized database of complaints.
* Enable municipal authorities to manage and assign complaints.
* Improve transparency in the complaint-resolution process.
* Reduce manual complaint-handling processes.
* Provide better monitoring and management of civic issues.

---

## 👥 Proposed Users

### 👤 Citizen

Citizens will be able to:

* Register and log in.
* Submit a new complaint.
* Select a complaint category.
* Provide complaint details.
* Add location information.
* Upload supporting images/evidence.
* Track complaint status.
* View previous complaints.

### 🏢 Municipal Official / Administrator

Municipal officials will be able to:

* Log in to the administrative system.
* View submitted complaints.
* Filter and categorize complaints.
* Assign complaints to the appropriate department.
* Update complaint status.
* Add remarks or updates.
* Monitor pending and resolved complaints.
* Manage complaints through a centralized dashboard.
